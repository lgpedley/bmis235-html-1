The brain is the the central control organ of the human nervous system It weighs about 3 pounds and has roughly 90 billion working neurons

Our brains develope across our lifetime. It is every changing.

Three facts about The brain

  • Your brain generates about 12 to 25 watts of electricity while you are awake.
  • The brain tissue itself cannot feel pain because it lacks pain receptors.
  • Your eyes and brain can process an image you have seen for as little as 13 milliseconds.
